I build a financial planning product. Most days that means a .NET backend, an Angular frontend, a cube engine that has to answer questions over hundreds of millions of cells, and a SQL database that occasionally reminds me who is in charge.

This blog is where I write down the things I had to learn the hard way. Performance rounds that took a query from minutes to a second. Production incidents and what actually caused them. Design decisions that looked obvious and were not.

Each post will follow the same shape: the problem, what I tried, the numbers, and what I would do differently.
